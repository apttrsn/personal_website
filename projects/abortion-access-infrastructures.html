Abortion Access Infrastructures

My dissertation examines how grassroots abortion organizations and the technologies that support, surveil, or fail them relate to one another in the Midwest United States after Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization (2022). It draws on interviews, ethnography, and co-design with more than a dozen abortion organizations, including the Chicago Abortion Fund and Exhale Pro-Voice, and on critical care theories that understand intimacy through care and relationships instead of privacy and taboo.
