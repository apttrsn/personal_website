Adrian Petterson

I am a doctoral candidate at the Faculty of Information at the University of Toronto, working in feminist human computer interaction (HCI) and science and technology studies (STS). My research looks at how care, technology, and design come together in abortion access. I also volunteer as a counsellor with , an after-abortion support textline.
