Care Ethics in HCI

This project asks how care is conceptualized in human computer interaction. Our literature review offers a taxonomy of care conceptualizations, a diagrammatic tool for care research, and a critique of the field's narrow theoretical base. I extend it through studies of care among Latino house cleaners in Toronto who work in technology-mediated labour markets.
